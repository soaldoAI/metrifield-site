How it works

From field observation to a decision with the cost comparison attached

01

Drone screening

Drones fly scheduled and event-triggered RGB and thermal surveys over the array. Edge AI checks image quality, locates each module and grades anomalies on site. Terrain, shading, climate and on-site exposure data separate regional conditions from microclimates inside the array.

02

Robot terahertz diagnosis

A robot moves onto the flagged modules, and onto representative healthy ones, and scans them at close range with terahertz. This gives evidence of material layers and interface condition inside the encapsulation that surface imagery and thermal cannot show.

03

Cloud intelligence

Inspections, healthy controls, SCADA and electrical data, work orders and actual expenditure are linked through module IDs, locations, timestamps and manufacturing batches, then coupled with yield, thermal, degradation and cost models to form module, string and plant digital twins.

04

Decision support and feedback

Cleaning, reinspection, repair and replacement are prioritised by recoverable energy, expected loss and intervention cost. Each action's cost, condition change and generation response feeds back into the models.

Why it matters

Modules from the same factory can look identical on day one and age very differently in the field. The difference is traceable, if the data is connected.
