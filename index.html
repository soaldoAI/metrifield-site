How it works

From inspection to informed action.

01

Intelligent screening

Drone-mounted edge AI checks RGB and thermal image quality, maps observations to individual modules and prioritises anomalies for targeted inspection.

02

Targeted THz diagnostics

The wheel–rotor platform scans priority modules and healthy controls, using terahertz to assess encapsulation layers and buried interfaces in compatible module structures.

03

Spatiotemporal modelling

Inspection, exposure and electrical data update module–string–plant models. ST-Twins track condition across sites and time, identifying where further measurements could improve decisions.

04

Decisions and feedback

Owners compare maintenance and replacement options by expected net benefit. Actual costs and generation responses refine the models, improving future recommendations and inspection priorities.

  1. 01Screen

    Aerial screening

    Scheduled and event-triggered RGB and thermal surveys identify and locate anomalies. Module-level mapping, terrain and environmental data provide spatial context and reveal changes over time.

  2. 02Diagnose

    Targeted diagnostics

    Robotic close-range inspections examine selected anomalies alongside healthy reference modules. Where suitable, terahertz sensing adds subsurface evidence beyond conventional optical and thermal imaging.

  3. 03Model

    Spatiotemporal digital twin

    Inspection records, environmental exposure, electrical data and maintenance history are connected through asset IDs and timestamps to track degradation, estimate energy losses and quantify uncertainty.

  4. 04Optimise

    Decision intelligence

    Cleaning, reinspection, repair, replacement and deferral are evaluated against recoverable energy, cost and risk. Measured intervention outcomes refine future predictions and operating decisions.
