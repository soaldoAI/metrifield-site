Capabilities

Reduce today's losses. Prevent tomorrow's.

What we do for solar assets at each stage of their life: plants already operating, plants being designed and built, and the factories that make the modules. Not every anomaly warrants intervention. We assess the cost of inaction, prioritise by expected net benefit, and verify the outcomes, turning measured results into better decisions and fewer recurring losses.

01 / Operating plants

Recover energy. Avoid unnecessary work.

A detected anomaly is not automatically a work order. We combine existing plant data with scalable screening, escalating to detailed diagnostics only when further evidence could change a decision. Interventions are ranked by expected energy recovery, cost and risk, then evaluated against measured outcomes.

  • Distinguish actionable losses from environmental and operational effects.
  • Match diagnostic effort to the value of the decision.
  • Verify recovery and use the results to refine future priorities.

Decisions supportedClean · Reinspect · Repair · Replace · Defer

02 / Deploying plants

Avoid costs before they are built in.

Losses observed in operating plants reveal risks that conventional yield models may overlook. We translate comparable field evidence into site-specific design scenarios, evaluating material suitability, configuration and maintainability against lifetime energy and cost. Key assumptions remain traceable for validation once the plant is operational.

  • Identify degradation risks under local environmental conditions.
  • Compare design alternatives by lifetime value, not upfront cost alone.
  • Carry design assumptions into commissioning and performance monitoring.

Decisions supportedModule selection · Layout · Mounting · Maintenance access

03 / Manufacturing plants

Prevent recurring failures at the source.

Recurring field failures can reveal opportunities to improve materials and production. By comparing performance across matched environments and batches, we investigate potential links to material selection and process conditions. Targeted validation then supports changes that can reduce future defects, rework and warranty exposure.

  • Separate environmental effects from material and batch variation.
  • Focus testing on suspected causes with the greatest economic impact.
  • Validate corrective measures before wider production changes.

Decisions supportedMaterial specifications · Process optimisation · Batch QA · Product design
