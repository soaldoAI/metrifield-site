# Frequently asked questions

## What does Metrifield do in one sentence?
We inspect solar farms with drones and robots, look inside the modules with terahertz sensing, and run a digital twin that tells owners which action — clean, repair, replace or defer — pays back.

## How is this different from a normal drone thermal inspection?
A standard inspection gives you a list of anomalies. We add three things: edge AI that ranks anomalies in flight; terahertz diagnostics that show subsurface material condition cameras cannot see; and a digital twin that links each module's materials, exposure and history to decide whether action is worth it, with the cost comparison made explicit.

## What is a digital twin in your context?
A living record of each module — what it is made of, where it lives, what it has been exposed to, what inspections found and how it has performed — combined with a physics-and-data model that predicts how it will degrade and what intervention is most economical. We call it a spatiotemporally resolved digital twin, or ST-Twin, because it tracks change across both the site and time.

## What is terahertz sensing and why does it matter?
Terahertz radiation sits between infrared and microwave. It passes through glass and encapsulant and is sensitive to moisture, delamination and material changes, so it reveals degradation inside a module before it shows up as a hotspot or a power loss.

## Do you fly the drones yourselves?
Yes. Surveys are operated by Metrifield with CASA-credentialed remote pilots. The lifecycle service uses automated, remotely operated surveys on a regular cadence.

## Which parts of Australia do you cover?
Australia-wide. Our regional model covers the tropical north, eastern seaboard, arid interior, inland agricultural margins, Mediterranean-type south-west and temperate south-east.

## How do we get started?
With a focused pilot: one site, a defined scope and a measured baseline. If the recommended actions deliver measured value, we scale to the portfolio.

## How long does a pilot take?
It depends on site size and scope; we agree the timeline during scoping. Contact us for specifics.

## What do we receive after a survey?
A prioritised inspection plan ranked by recoverable value, module-level anomaly maps, a georeferenced orthomosaic, a 3D site model, and — for the lifecycle service — an ongoing dashboard.

## Do you need our SCADA or monitoring data?
It helps. Inverter, string and weather data improve the twin's estimates of recoverable energy, but a pilot can start with inspection data alone.

## Can you help with new plant design?
Yes. We translate field evidence from comparable operating plants into site-specific scenarios for module selection, layout, tilt, row spacing, mounting height and maintenance access, ranked by whole-of-life cost.

## Do you work with module manufacturers?
Yes. We trace field failure modes back to bills of materials, supplier batches and lamination windows, and help validate corrective measures.

## Can you build a custom computer-vision model for us?
Yes — dataset design, labelling, training, quantisation and deployment on NVIDIA Jetson, Hailo or similar edge hardware, on drones, ground robots or fixed cameras.

## Can you inspect after hail or a storm?
Yes. We map the event footprint and safe access first, then RGB damage mapping, then qualified electrical and structural review; thermal is used only under valid conditions.
