# Who Metrifield works with

## Owners and operators of solar farms, and O&M providers (operating plants)
Not every anomaly warrants intervention. We combine existing plant data with scalable screening, escalating to detailed diagnostics only when further evidence could change a decision. Decisions supported: clean, reinspect, repair, replace, defer. Outcomes: recovered energy, less low-value work, verified repairs.

## Developers and EPCs (plants being designed and built)
Losses observed in operating plants reveal risks conventional yield models overlook. We translate comparable field evidence into site-specific design scenarios — module selection, layout, tilt, row spacing, mounting height, maintenance access — ranked by whole-of-life cost rather than first-year yield. Design assumptions are carried into commissioning and performance monitoring.

## Module manufacturers
Recurring field failures reveal opportunities to improve materials and production. By comparing performance across matched environments and batches we separate environmental effects from material and batch variation, focus testing on suspected causes with the greatest economic impact, and validate corrective measures before wider production changes. Decisions supported: material specifications, process optimisation, batch QA, product design.

## Research partners and engineers
We collaborate with universities and research organisations on materials diagnostics, terahertz sensing and degradation modelling.

## Target customers
Our initial focus is owners and operators of small-to-medium solar farms across Australia. We also work with developers, EPCs and module manufacturers.
