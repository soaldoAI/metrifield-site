# Services

## Drone surveys
Drone-mounted RGB and thermal cameras capture every module in an array. An on-board edge inference unit detects and ranks anomalies in flight — hotspots, cracked glass, soiling, vegetation, string outages — so the customer receives a prioritised inspection plan rather than raw imagery. Each survey also produces a georeferenced orthomosaic and a 3D model (digital twin) of the site. Operations are by CASA-credentialed remote pilots under a certified operator that holds public liability, professional indemnity and workers' compensation insurance; certificates of currency are provided during scoping. Typical uses: commissioning inspection, annual condition survey, post-event damage mapping (hail, storm, fire), warranty evidence.

## Subsurface diagnostics (terahertz)
Where screening flags a module worth a closer look, a panel-crawling robot carries a terahertz (THz) sensor across its surface. Terahertz penetrates glass and encapsulant and returns material-state evidence — delamination, moisture ingress, encapsulant and interface degradation — that RGB and thermal imaging can only infer. Repeat scans quantify change between visits and verify repairs. Typical uses: root cause of underperforming strings, warranty claims, pre-purchase due diligence. The robot is placed on the panel; it does not navigate the site autonomously.

## Custom edge AI
The same pipeline that powers our surveys is available for customers' own use cases. We collect and label data, train computer-vision models, quantise them for edge hardware and deploy them on drones, ground robots or fixed cameras, with on-device inference and store-and-forward of only the events that matter. Deployment targets include NVIDIA Jetson and Hailo accelerators. Typical uses: vegetation encroachment, security and intrusion, fire and smoke detection, construction progress, wildlife monitoring.

## Lifecycle digital twin (ongoing service)
Automated, remotely operated surveys on a regular cadence keep the twin current. Each module's materials, exposure history, inspection evidence and energy performance are linked in one record, and the twin predicts the most economical action — clean, reinspect, repair, replace or defer — with the cost comparison made explicit. Customers get a decision dashboard with recoverable-energy and cost estimates. Metrifield operates the drones and robots; hardware and software are delivered together as one service, not sold separately.

## How an engagement runs
1. Focused pilot — one site, defined scope, measured baseline; usually a survey plus targeted diagnostics on flagged modules.
2. Validated value — recommended actions are carried out and the recovery is measured against the baseline.
3. Fleet rollout — regular automated surveys across the portfolio feeding the twin and O&M planning.
Pricing is quoted per engagement after a conversation about the site; we do not publish a price list.
