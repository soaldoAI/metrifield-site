# About Metrifield Dynamics

## Who we are
Metrifield Dynamics Pty Ltd is a Sydney-based company providing lifecycle intelligence for solar assets — operating solar farms and plants still being designed and built. We combine robotic field inspection (drones and panel-crawling robots), multi-modal sensing (RGB, thermal, LiDAR and terahertz) and a spatiotemporally resolved digital twin to turn field evidence into maintenance, design and manufacturing decisions. ## Mission
Our ambition is to increase the lifetime value of solar investments — more productive assets, better deployment choices and more durable products — by turning Australia's diverse operating conditions into an industry advantage. Over time our database becomes a map of how solar hardware ages across Australia's climate zones: the first climate database of solar farms in Australia.
